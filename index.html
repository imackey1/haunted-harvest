What is happening on Main Street?
  • People: a witch, a vampire and a sheet-ghost go trick-or-treating while a zombie shuffles the other way.
  • Animals: a black cat blinks on the fence, a dog in a pumpkin vest trots beside the witch, bats flap across the moon.
  • Weather: low fog drifts through the street and windblown leaves tumble past.
  • Vehicle and objects: a hearse with spinning wheels and glowing headlights; a paper lantern swings from the shop awning.
  • Halloween: jack-o'-lanterns you can light (press them or tab to them), a full moon, and flickering windows.

What is happening in the boutique?
  • People: a shopkeeper waves from behind the counter while two customers wander the floor in their costumes.
  • Animals: a black cat flicks its tail on the counter, bats sway from the beam, and a spider lowers itself on a thread.
  • Weather: rain streaks the window and lightning flickers across the glass.
  • Vehicle and objects: a taxi with glowing headlights passes the window; a ceiling fan spins and costumes sway on the rack.

Rooftops

High above the city, a witch flies by the moon and a rooftop party shakes under the storm clouds.

Down to Main Street Climb down to the Main Street scene
What is happening on the rooftops?
  • People: a skeleton and a pumpkin-headed guest dance, and a young stargazer watches the sky; a rider waves from the balloon basket.
  • Animals: an owl blinks on the chimney, a cat prowls the ridge and bats circle the moon.
  • Weather: storm clouds roll across the moon, lightning flickers and the wind pushes the bunting and leaves.
  • Vehicle and objects: a witch swoops by on her broom, a pumpkin balloon drifts across the sky, and the weather vane swings.
  • Halloween: a huge harvest moon, party bunting, a jack-o'-lantern to light, and chimney smoke curling in the night.
